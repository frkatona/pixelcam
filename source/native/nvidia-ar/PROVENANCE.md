# NVIDIA AR SDK source provenance

The files in include/ and src/ are copied unmodified from NVIDIA's public [Maxine-AR-SDK repository](https://github.com/NVIDIA-Maxine/Maxine-AR-SDK), revision `f54db7ecaa0534b696d6194ac7252fc39e4d94a1`, version 0.8.7.0, downloaded October 22, 2026. The upstream MIT license is retained in LICENSE.txt and in the source headers.

NVIDIA's runtime, dependencies, and model packages are not included here. Setup-EyeContact.ps1 obtains the separately licensed AR 0.8.7 Ampere redistributable from NVIDIA's official download center and preserves its EULA and third-party notices in the installation directory.
